365 Days Tracker

365 Days Tracker is a browser-based daily progress tracker for habits, hobbies, learning, skills and personal goals. Create a tracker, choose a date range, mark each day and look back at your progress over time.

How it works

Start small. Keep showing up.

  1. Open the tracker in your browser.
  2. Create a tracker for something you want to work on.
  3. Mark dates and add notes as you go.
  4. Use the calendar and statistics to reflect on your progress.

No account or sign-up is required to use the current version.

Privacy by design

Your tracker data stays in your browser

The current app stores tracker information in browser storage on the device and browser profile where you use it. It uses local browser storage and an IndexedDB recovery copy where supported. The app does not send tracker entries to its own application server.

Keep your own backup.

Browser storage is not a substitute for a separate backup. Clearing browser data, changing browsers or devices, or losing access to a browser profile can make locally stored information unavailable. Use the app's backup feature and keep the exported file somewhere safe. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to view your trackers.

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers

Is 365 Days Tracker free to use?

The current hosted version is available to use without a sign-up or payment.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The current version works in your browser without an account.

Can I use it on another device?

Yes, you can open the website on another device, but browser-stored tracker data does not automatically sync between devices. Use the backup and restore features to transfer your data.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Your locally stored tracker information may be lost. Download and keep backups regularly.
